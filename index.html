Automation and AI

AI and everyday tools wired into daily operations

Problem
Support and operations teams at a fast-growing global platform jumped between Slack, Zendesk, Google Docs and internal tools, repeating the same copy-paste work every day.
What I did
Built integrations that connected those tools to the company's systems, including OpenAI and BigQuery.
Outcome
Teams worked from the tools they already used, without switching between systems or re-typing data.

APIs and integrations

A monolith split into services, without stopping the business

Problem
A large monolith slowed every release down. Teams kept stepping on each other's changes, and deploys were risky.
What I did
Extracted parts of it into separate services that communicate through Kafka, added API contract tests to every build, and moved the services into lean, fast-building Docker containers.
Outcome
Teams could release their services independently, with breaking changes caught before they reached production.
