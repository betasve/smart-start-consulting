How to contact us about your data: use the contact form and start your message with "Privacy request". We will reply to the email address you enter in the form.

2. What data is collected and why

Contact form

When you send a message, we receive your email address, your message, and technical data such as the time of sending. The form is processed by Formspree.

  • Purpose: to reply to your enquiry and, if you wish, to prepare an offer.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in replying to messages (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: up to 2 years after our last contact. If we start working together, data needed for contracts and invoices is kept as long as Bulgarian accounting and tax law requires.

Booking a call

Calls are booked through Cal.com. The booking calendar is loaded from Cal.com only when you click a "Book a free call" button. When you book, we receive your name, email address, the time you picked and anything you write in the booking notes. The call itself takes place on Google Meet, using the link in your booking confirmation. Cal.com may set the cookies it needs for the booking to work, under its own privacy policy.

  • Purpose: to schedule and hold the call, and to prepare for it.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: up to 2 years after our last contact, as for the contact form.

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website: which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive (e.g. from a search engine or an ad), approximate location (country and city), device and browser type, and clicks on buttons such as "Book a free call". Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 4a of the Bulgarian Electronic Communications Act).
  • Retention: up to 14 months.
  • Withdrawing consent: click "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page and turn analytics off. Analytics cookies are then deleted.

WhatsApp and Viber

The WhatsApp and Viber buttons open those apps. If you write to us there, we receive your phone number, name and messages, which we use only to reply to you. Those apps process your data under their own privacy policies.

Hosting and website files

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts and icons are hosted on this website itself. The cookie banner script is loaded from jsDelivr. When your browser loads it, the provider receives your IP address and browser information, which is technically necessary to deliver it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to its processing;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing done before you withdrew it.

To use these rights, send us a "Privacy request" through the contact form. We will reply within one month.

You can also file a complaint with the Bulgarian data protection authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни), www.cpdp.bg.

6. Automated decisions

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.
